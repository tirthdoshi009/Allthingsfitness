# Bigger Leaner Stronger

- **Author:** Michael Matthews
- **Category:** Training / Nutrition
- **Status:** Reading
- **Rating:** ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️☆

## Why I picked it up

I wanted one opinionated, beginner-friendly plan instead of ten conflicting opinions from the
internet.

## Big ideas

1. **Progressive overload is the whole game.** Strength comes from adding weight or reps over
   time. If the numbers in your log are not moving, the programme is not working, no matter how
   sweaty the session felt.
2. **Compound lifts first.** Squat, deadlift, bench and overhead press give the most return per
   minute spent. Isolation work is a garnish, not the meal.
3. **Calories decide weight, protein protects muscle.** Whether you gain or lose is set by energy
   balance; adequate protein and lifting decide whether the change is muscle or not.
4. **Boring beats clever.** A simple plan followed for a year outperforms a sophisticated one
   abandoned in six weeks.
5. **Track something.** Weights, reps and body weight are cheap to record and make progress
   arguable rather than a feeling.

## What I'm actually going to do

- [ ] Run a three-day upper/lower/full-body split for the next eight weeks.
- [ ] Log every set and add weight only when all target reps are clean.
- [ ] Hit a consistent daily protein target rather than micromanaging every meal.

## Where it didn't convince me

The supplement discussion feels more confident than the evidence warrants, and the tone assumes a
reader whose main constraint is effort. Mine is usually time.

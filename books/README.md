**Physiology and endurance**
- Training for the Uphill Athlete, House, Johnston, Jornet
- The Science of Running, Steve Magness
- Exercise Physiology, McArdle, Katch, Katch (textbook)

**Movement, mobility, rehab**
- Becoming a Supple Leopard, Kelly Starrett
- Anatomy Trains, Thomas Myers (read critically)

**Recovery, sleep, nutrition**
- Why We Sleep, Matthew Walker (worth reading, worth also reading the criticism)
- Burn, Herman Pontzer

**Mind and performance**
- The Inner Game of Tennis, Timothy Gallwey
- Peak, Anders Ericsson and Robert Pool
- The Art of Learning, Josh Waitzkin
